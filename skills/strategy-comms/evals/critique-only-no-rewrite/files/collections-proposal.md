Hi Marguerite,

I wanted to share a proposal the finance ops team has been working on. As you know, late payments have been a growing problem and everyone agrees the current reminder process is broken. Right now reminders are sent by hand by two analysts working from a spreadsheet, and things fall through the cracks constantly.

We've looked at three vendors and think Tallybridge is the best fit. It automates reminders, has a customer portal, and other companies our size have seen DSO improve by 10 to 15 days. Our DSO is 58 days today against a target of 45, so this would basically close the gap.

The cost is $84K a year plus a one-time implementation fee. Implementation takes about 10 weeks and needs someone from IT for the ERP integration, which we haven't scoped with them yet. We'd also be able to move one of the two analysts onto the month-end close, which is badly understaffed.

One thing to flag is that our top 15 customers account for most of the overdue balance and they're on negotiated terms, so automated reminders may not change their behaviour much. But we think the long tail is where the volume is.

We'd love to get your support for this so we can move forward. Happy to walk through the details whenever works.
