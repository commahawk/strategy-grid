# Data Quality Program Update — Q4 Steering Committee

## Slide 1: Agenda

## Slide 2: Background
- DQ program started in Q1 after the March regulatory reporting restatement
- 3 workstreams: monitoring, ownership, remediation

## Slide 3: Monitoring Workstream
- Deployed automated checks on 212 of 340 critical data elements (CDEs)
- 1,840 checks running daily
- Detection time for DQ issues went from 4.2 days avg to 0.6 days

## Slide 4: Ownership Workstream
- 290 of 340 CDEs have a named data owner
- Remaining 50 are in Commercial; reorg delayed assignments
- Data owner training completed by 74%

## Slide 5: Remediation Workstream
- 96 open DQ issues at start of year, 131 now
- 58 issues closed YTD, 93 new issues found (because monitoring is finding more)
- Avg age of open issues 71 days; 19 issues older than 180 days
- Remediation is funded out of each LOB's run budget, no dedicated capacity
